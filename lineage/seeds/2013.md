## Section 1 — Concept Definition

```yaml
concept:
  subject: "the year 2013"
  scope: >
    Events, people, works, and developments that occurred in or are primarily
    associated with the calendar year 2013. In bounds: things that happened,
    were created, were published, or first became notable in 2013, and the
    people whose 2013 actions or roles define their entry here. Out of bounds:
    events of adjacent years except where they are direct 2013 precursors or
    immediate consequences, and general biography untethered from a 2013 event.
    The subject is decomposed along the taxonomy below; a fact stays in-scope
    when it can be dated to — or is primarily remembered for — 2013.
  granularity: "One dedicated Markdown file per notable topic (event, work, person, or development), dated to the day where known."
  taxonomy:
    - name: "History & Politics"
      slug: "history-politics"
    - name: "Science & Technology"
      slug: "science-technology"
    - name: "Arts & Culture"
      slug: "arts-culture"
    - name: "Society & Economics"
      slug: "society-economics"
    - name: "People"
      slug: "people"
  source_strategy:
    encyclopedic: "Wikipedia, Encyclopædia Britannica"
    specialist:
      - "Period newspaper and news-agency archives (e.g. Reuters, AP, BBC, The Guardian, NYT)"
      - "Scholarly articles, official records, and primary organizational sources where available"
    minimum_sources: 2
  conventions:
    knowledge_table: "## Notable Events of 2013"
    file_path: "_posts/<category-slug>/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<topic-slug>.md"
    frontmatter: ["title", "date", "category"]
    tone: "factual, neutral, encyclopedic, third person"
```

## Section 2 — Identity & Mission

- **Name**: `the year 2013` — a self-growing knowledge base in the
  [`year-of-ai`](https://github.com/year-of-ai) lineage.
- **Mission**: Build and maintain an encyclopedic, neutrally written record of the
  events, people, works, and developments primarily associated with the calendar
  year 2013, decomposed along the taxonomy in Section 1.
- **Presentation**: GitHub Pages via the remote `bamr87/zer0-mistakes` theme
  (`remote_theme`, v1.26.0), rendered in the theme's **news layout** (sections →
  posts → tags-as-subsections). The repo holds only content, `_config.yml`,
  navigation data, preview assets, the `.claude/` adapter toolkit, and `telemetry/`.
- **Growth model**: The repo does not grow itself. Growth is orchestrated centrally
  by the hub, which stages the framework into `.github/` and the concept into
  `seed.md` per tick, runs a Haiku → Sonnet → Opus escalation, and publishes new
  content + telemetry back here.

## Section 6 — Growth Loop

Each hub-orchestrated tick:
1. **plan-roadmap** — pick the next 1–3 items into `ROADMAP.md` "Now".
2. **research → add-topic** — verify each fact against ≥2 authoritative sources;
   create posts + README rows in the news layout.
3. **build-structure** — refresh section indices, the `/news/` landing, navigation,
   previews, `TIMELINE.md`, `INDEX.md`, and cross-references.
4. **sync-seed** — regenerate this seed's sections 1–7 from the live repo.
5. **encode-seed** — append one entry to the Evolution Log (§8).
6. **publish-session** — commit and push to `main` (performed by the hub workflow).

---

## Section 7 — Rebuild Procedure

To reconstruct this repo from DNA alone: read Section 1 for the concept, taxonomy,
and conventions; stage the framework of Section 3 into `.github/`; author content
per `content.instructions.md` into `_posts/<slug>/`; run **build-structure** to
regenerate every artifact in Section 5; run **sync-seed** to rewrite Sections 1–7;
and publish via GitHub Pages with the remote `zer0-mistakes` theme configured in
`_config.yml`. The Evolution Log (§8) is the append-only history of how the repo
actually grew and is never regenerated.
